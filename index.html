About Me

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Ho Chi Minh University of Technology (HCMUT). My research interests lie in Computer Graphics, Computer Vision and Applied Artificial Intelligence to solve real-world problems in domains such as traffic management and medical healthcare.

Previously, I was a Software Engineer Intern at NEC Vietnam and a Research Intern at the Visual Media Laboratory at Kyoto Institute of Technology.

This page showcases my work, publications and personal projects created in my free time. Feel free to reach out via my contact info if you would like to collaborate or connect!
